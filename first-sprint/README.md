**Learning objective:** By the end of this lesson, students will be able to choose a small group of related stories and use their board to track progress.

## Choose a small first goal

Your board now contains several features. We need to choose where to begin.

For this exercise, plan your first sprint as **the first two project workdays**.

Write one sentence describing the result you want by the end.

For our matching game:

```text
Sprint 1 goal:
The player can read the rules and reveal cards on the board.
```

This moves us toward our MVP. It does not complete the whole game.

## Select stories that support the goal

For this example, we could select:

- Read the instructions.
- See the face-down cards.
- Select a card to reveal it.

Move the selected cards from **Product Backlog** into **Sprint**.

Keep the other stories in **Product Backlog**, including MVP stories planned for later.

> 💡 **MVP** describes the first complete version of your game. A **sprint** is a fixed period of work toward a goal. Your MVP may take more than one sprint.

## Update the board as you work

| When this happens | Move the card to |
| ----------------- | ---------------- |
| You select the story for the current sprint | **Sprint** |
| You begin building the feature | **Doing** |
| The feature works, you have tested it, and you have committed and pushed the code | **Done** |

Try to keep one story in **Doing** at a time.

Writing a story does not make its feature complete. During today’s planning session, **Doing** and **Done** can stay empty.

At the end of the sprint, review what works and use that information to choose your next group of stories.

## 🎓 You Do: Prepare your first sprint

Choose **two or three related MVP stories** that you can reasonably work on during the first two project workdays.

Move them into **Sprint**. Add your sprint goal and planned start and end dates to your repository’s README.

## Save and check your plan

Upload `wireframe.png` to your repository. If you already work locally, save it in the repository, commit it, and push.

Add a planning section to your README:

```md
## Project Planning

- [Project board](PASTE-YOUR-BOARD-URL-HERE)
- [Wireframe](./wireframe.png)

### Sprint 1

Goal: [Describe the result you want.]

Start: [Date]
End: [Date]
```
