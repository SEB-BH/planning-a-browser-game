**Learning objective:** By the end of this lesson, students will be able to access the tools needed to plan their project.

## Prepare your workspace

You should have chosen your game and read its project requirements before this workshop.

1. Sign in to [GitHub](https://github.com).
2. Open the repository you will use for Project 1.
3. If you have not created it yet, create a **public repository** on your personal account. Give it a name related to your game and include a README.
4. Open [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com) in another browser tab.
5. Keep your project requirements available.

Confirm that you can open your repository and draw a rectangle in Excalidraw.

> 💡 Keep the same repository throughout the project so your work and commit history stay together.
